# Swing Trade Screen — June 2026

**Date:** 9 June 2026 | **Market:** NSE | **Goal:** ~5% per trade | **Filters:** strong positive sentiment + price momentum + rising volume

---

## Bottom line first

The honest answer this month is **one tactical idea, and only with tight risk control — not a basket.** The broad tape is working against momentum trades right now, and most of the names that screen well on sentiment and volume have **already run too far** to offer a clean 5% with acceptable downside. I'd rather give you a short, defensible list than pad it with chasers.

### Why I'm cautious on the overall setup

- **Nifty 50 closed 23,123 (−1.04%) on 8 June**, breaching 23,150 intraday. The structure is **bearish-to-sideways**, with a defined floor near 23,000 and resistance at 23,250–23,300.
- Two macro overhangs: **escalation in West Asia** and a **hot US jobs print** (NFP 139K vs 85K expected), both feeding risk-off flows.
- In a tape like this, momentum names tend to give sharp, fast pullbacks. That cuts directly against a "buy strength, target +5%" plan unless the entry is disciplined.

This is the kind of month where being selective matters more than finding ideas.

## How to play it

1. **One name, not five.** Paras Defence, on a pullback to ₹940–950, +5% target ~₹990–1,000, stop ₹915.
2. **If Paras doesn't pull back, do nothing.** Buying at ₹984 after a 20% week is exactly the entry this tape punishes.
3. **If the Nifty loses 23,000 decisively, stand aside entirely.** In a confirmed risk-off leg, even good momentum names bleed, and no single-stock thesis is worth fighting the index.

Per your own brief: if nothing's worth it, I won't force a name — and three of the five candidates were rejected on exactly that logic. Paras is the only one I'd put in front of you, and only with the entry discipline above.
