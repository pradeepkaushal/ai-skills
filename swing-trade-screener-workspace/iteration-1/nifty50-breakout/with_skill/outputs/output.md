## Verdict: No qualifying swing setups today (2026-06-09)

I scanned the Nifty 50 specifically for stocks **breaking out with strong volume**. On a down day, a handful of defensive/relative-strength names held up, and two are genuinely near their 52-week highs — but **none cleared all four gates (Momentum, Volume, Sentiment, Trade-Viability).** The single thing the request hinges on — a *breakout confirmed by expanding volume* — is exactly what's missing. In a tape where the index is below its 50-DMA with 3 stocks falling for every 1 rising, buying "strength" is buying into a current that drags everything down. Forcing a trade here has poor odds.

The one name with real momentum, **Apollo Hospitals**, is worth a watchlist slot: if it **digests the 24-Jun EGM**, then **pulls back toward the ₹8,000–8,180 zone (prior breakout / 20-SMA) and bases there on contracting volume, then turns up on a genuine ≥1.5× volume surge**, it would be a far cleaner, lower-risk entry than chasing it today.

**Position sizing reminder (for when a setup does qualify):** risk only ~1–2% of capital per trade. Size = (capital × risk%) ÷ (entry − stop). The ~5%/month goal is a portfolio outcome of disciplined trades, not a per-name guarantee — and the discipline that matters most today is *not trading*.
