## 1. Market context (this is the most important part)

You asked which Nifty 50 stocks are "breaking out with strong volume right now." The honest answer is: **almost none, in the classic sense.** The market is in a corrective, risk-off phase today, and chasing breakouts into this tape is how people get chopped up. Here is the backdrop as of 9 June 2026:

|---|---|---|
| Nifty 50 spot | ~23,120–23,155 (June 8 close 23,123, -1.04%) | Down ~12% from the 5 Jan 2026 high of 26,373 |

| Macro triggers | Iran–Israel tension, rising crude, FII net selling (~₹5,556 cr) | Risk-off; news-driven gaps likely |
| Key support / resistance | Support 23,000 (heavy put OI) then 22,900; resistance 23,267 → 23,500–23,550 | A break below 23,000 accelerates downside |

**Implication for breakout trading:** In a market trading below its key moving averages with weak breadth, the base rate for breakouts failing (false breakouts / "bull traps") is high. So instead of hunting fresh 52-week-high breakouts (there are very few among large caps, and the few that exist — ITDC, Adani Green, Paras Defence — are mostly outside the Nifty 50), the higher-probability play is **relative strength**: the handful of Nifty 50 names that are holding up, grinding higher, and seeing buying *while everything around them falls.* Those are in defensives — pharma, healthcare and FMCG.

I've turned the two cleanest of those into proper trade plans, plus a watchlist of "almost there" names. Every plan assumes a hard rule: **if Nifty closes below 23,000, stand aside / exit momentum longs.**

## 4. How to actually trade this (rules, not vibes)

1. **Respect the regime.** Below the weekly EMAs with weak breadth, keep gross exposure light. This is a "2 small positions" day, not an "all-in on breakouts" day.
2. **Wait for the trigger.** Both plans are *conditional* on a confirming close above the trigger level on decent volume. No trigger = no trade. Do not pre-empt.
3. **Hard market filter:** If **Nifty closes below 23,000**, cancel new longs and tighten/exit existing momentum positions — that's the level where selling accelerates.
4. **Size for the stop, not the conviction.** Risk ~1% of capital per trade. With a ~3.7% stop on Sun Pharma, that defines your share count.
5. **Book partials at T1, trail the rest.** Don't be greedy in a corrective market — take money off the table into strength.
6. **Volume is the tell.** A breakout without above-average volume in *this* tape is guilty until proven innocent.
