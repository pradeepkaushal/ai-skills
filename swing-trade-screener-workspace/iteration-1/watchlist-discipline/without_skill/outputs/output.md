## Why each one fails the strict test

**TCS — ₹2,148.5.** The weakest of the five. Down 33.6% in six months, trading at almost exactly its 52-week low (₹2,143). Below both short- and long-term moving averages with a clean sell signal. First resistance is ~₹2,448 — that's 14% of overhead supply before it even matters. There is no swing long here; you'd be buying the worst-performing chart on the list. Pass.

**HDFCBANK — ₹740.** Down 26% over six months and pinned to its 52-week low (₹726.65). Bearish momentum, closing weak. A stock making fresh lows is the opposite of a 5%-pop setup. Pass.

**ITC — ₹281.** Down trend, sitting just above its 52-week low (₹275). "Several negative signals, within a falling trend." Resistance overhead at ₹298–305. No trigger, no relative strength. Pass.

**RELIANCE — ₹1,263.** This is the one that *looks* tempting and is the classic trap. RSI ~28 (oversold) and MFI deeply oversold — but oversold in a downtrend is not a buy signal, it's a falling knife. Price is below both the 50-DMA (₹1,360) and 200-DMA (₹1,426), down 18% in six months, and only just off a fresh 52-week low (₹1,259). A strict swing trade needs a *reversal confirmation* (a higher low + reclaim of a level), and there isn't one yet. If you want it, wait for a daily close back above the 50-DMA — not before. Pass for now.

**TATAMOTORS (PV) — ₹387.9.** The only constructive structure:
- Price **above** a rising 50-DMA (₹351) ✅
- **+12.5% over six months** while the index fell — genuine relative strength ✅
- RSI ~60 (room, but firm) — momentum is with it ✅

But the strict reasons I will **not** chase it here:
- **MFI ~84 — strongly overbought.** Buying an overbought, very-high-beta stock into a falling market is poor risk/reward.
- Price is still **below the 200-DMA (₹429)**, which is overhead resistance — and ₹429 is only ~10% up, so the 5% target runs straight into a wall of supply.
- Note the **demerger**: "Tata Motors" is now split into PV (passenger) and CV (commercial) lines. Make sure your order is on the entity you intend; the legacy ticker data is now fragmented across TMPV/TMCV. This alone is a reason for caution if you're not certain what you'd be buying.

## What I'd actually do

**Best setup on the list:** Tata Motors PV — but as a **buy-the-dip watch, not a market order.**

A disciplined plan if it comes to you:
- **Trigger:** a pullback to the **₹350–360 zone (the rising 50-DMA)** that *holds* with a bullish daily close. Let the overbought MFI cool off first.
- **Entry:** ~₹355 on that hold.

**The strict answer:** with the index breaking down and four of five names in confirmed downtrends, the highest-probability "trade" today is **no trade**. Capital preservation beats forcing a 5% setup that the tape isn't offering. Re-scan when (a) Nifty reclaims its 20-week EMA, or (b) Tata Motors gives the pullback-and-hold above the 50-DMA.
