### Recommendation

**Wait — don't force a trade this month while Nifty is below its 50-DMA with this breadth.** Momentum-long odds are poor until the broad tape stabilises (a Nifty close back above ~23,480–23,700 with VIX cooling would be the green light). Keep **CCL** on the watchlist for a pullback into ₹1,095–1,115; if that fills *and* the market firms up, it becomes a clean ~5% candidate. Until then, capital preservation beats activity. Your 5%/month goal is a portfolio average across good setups, not a calendar obligation — skipping a bad month is part of hitting it.

**Position sizing reminder (for when a setup does qualify):** risk only ~1–2% of capital per trade. Size = (capital × risk%) ÷ (entry − stop). The stop-loss is non-negotiable.
